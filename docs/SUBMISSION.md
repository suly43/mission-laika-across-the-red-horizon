## Project summary

Mission Laika is a browser game for students ages 7–12. Players guide a robotic dog through space-station survival, landing preparation, a Mars surface expedition, and a lava-tunnel mission. They balance health and battery while choosing food, ventilation, power, and radiation shielding. Those choices carry forward as players collect surface records, take shelter, and decide when to use the tunnel flashlight. NASA space-weather records, archived imagery, and published Mars science give the activities real-world context.

## Project details

Space mission decisions have consequences that may only appear later. Mission Laika lets learners make those decisions in a game they can replay. An early power choice changes the energy available for surface work. Shielding consumes resources before it helps protect the rover in the game. A simulated signal delay demonstrates why Mission Control cannot respond immediately. On Mars, players weigh collecting six reference records against finding shelter and saving enough battery to return. The final tunnel mission adds a flashlight that helps find objectives while consuming energy.

Basic mode gives learners more time to read and explore. Advanced mode adds deadlines. Keyboard and touch inputs, mute, larger text, high contrast, reduced motion, and teacher notes support different ways of playing. The goal is to help students explain the reasoning behind a choice and test a different strategy on replay. The food activity focuses on safe selection and handling. The game does not model crop production.

The browser edition uses HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Canvas, Web Audio, and localStorage. A separate Python/Pygame edition provides an earlier, shorter take on the story. The game does not require custom hardware or an account.

## NASA data and resources

- **DONKI** activity counts for flares, coronal mass ejections, geomagnetic storms, and solar energetic particles inform a simplified risk setting. The game labels the archived fallback when live requests fail.
- **NASA Images** provides archived Mars reference records. The game also links NASA/JPL-Caltech rover galleries.
- **Curiosity RAD**, **Mars Relay Network**, NASA's Mars temperature references, and human-spaceflight resources inform the shielding, communications, cold exposure, food, exercise, and ventilation lessons.
- Battery, health, and risk values are game rules. The combined dust-storm and shield-damage rule simplifies distinct engineering risks.
