**BUILD A JUNIOR ASTRONAUT**\&#xA;**&#x20;MISSION TRAINER**

*Video Game Script*

# Characters and Communication Elements

LAIKA: Robotic companion. Guides the player, explains objectives, and communicates alerts.

SYSTEM: On-screen messages, instructions, timers, and action results.

PLAYER: Astronaut responsible for choosing resources and making decisions throughout the mission.

# Prologue — Welcome to the Mission

[SCENE: Inside the spacecraft. The camera shows the space station and then focuses on Laika.]

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x57;elcome, astronaut. Your mission is to support a scientific expedition to Mars. Every decision you make can affect your safety and the success of the mission.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x48;ello, astronaut! I'm Laika, your robotic companion. I'll be exploring Mars and collecting information. You'll need to help keep me safe and make sure we can complete our objectives.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x57;e'll investigate solar radiation, storms, environmental conditions, and possible signs of life. Are you ready to begin?

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x54;raining starts now. Complete all four levels to make it back home.

# Level 1 — Surviving on the Space Station

[SCENE: The player appears inside the station. A vending machine containing food and various objects is shown.]

## 1. Choosing the Food

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x42;efore we begin, we need to eat. Open the vending machine and choose the appropriate food. Remember: not everything that looks delicious is suitable for a space mission.

INFORMATION: Before the vending machine opens, a note should appear explaining that food must not be crunchy or crumbly, since it can leave crumbs, and drinks must be consumed using a straw.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x43;hoose the correct items: a sealed drink and chicken treated through irradiation to ensure its safety.

**LAIKA — IF THE CHOICE IS CORRECT:&#x20;**&#x45;xcellent choice! The food is ready to be stored and consumed safely.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x46;ood challenge completed.

**LAIKA — IF THE CHOICE IS INCORRECT:&#x20;**&#x43;areful! That choice is not suitable for our mission. Food and liquids must be handled properly inside the spacecraft.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x46;ood safety protocol violated. Restart the challenge.

[GAME NOTE: The trap items are a can of soda and a cookie. The correct choices specified in the document are a sealed drink and chicken treated with ionizing radiation.]

## 2. Cleaning the Station

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x47;ood job! Now we need to clean up. In microgravity, crumbs and tiny particles can float around the cabin and cause problems.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x55;se the vacuum cleaner to collect all the food debris you find on the panels.

INFORMATION: While cleaning, Laika should explain why this task is important:

“Eating in microgravity isn't always a piece of cake! Preparing food while floating is a challenge. Some foods can contaminate the surrounding air or the equipment.”

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x54;he station is clean! Keeping our space tidy is also part of staying safe.

## 3. Resting in a Safe Place

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x57;e need to rest before the next challenge. Choose where to attach your sleeping bag. Look for an area near a ventilation grille.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x43;hoose a location: the control area, the exercise area, or the sleeping quarters.

**LAIKA — IF THE LOCATION IS CORRECT:&#x20;**&#x47;ood decision. Ventilation helps remove the carbon dioxide we produce when we breathe.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x53;afe location selected. The resting sequence will begin automatically.

**LAIKA — IF THE LOCATION IS INCORRECT:&#x20;**&#x57;arning! Carbon dioxide may build up near you. We need to rest in a properly ventilated area.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x43;O₂ buildup risk. Choose another location.

[SCENE: A short animation shows the character securing their sleeping bag next to a ventilation grille.]

## 4. Exercising in Microgravity

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x52;ise and shine, astronaut! We need to exercise in space, too. A lack of physical activity in microgravity can lead to muscle loss and reduced bone density.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x42;efore using the exercise equipment, request a safety harness from the vending machine. Enter the code for the correct item.

**LAIKA — IF THE HARNESS IS SELECTED:&#x20;**&#x43;orrect! The harness will keep you secured to the equipment while you exercise.

**LAIKA — IF A TRAP ITEM IS SELECTED:&#x20;**&#x54;hat item won't help us. Look for the safety harness.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x46;asten the harness and complete the activity on the stationary bike or exercise equipment.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x54;raining completed! You're ready to move on.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x4C;evel 1 completed. Target time: 7 to 8 minutes.

[GAME NOTE: The player should not run freely in microgravity, because they could push off and collide with the ceiling. The vending machine may include distracting objects, such as a rubber duck.]

# Level 2 — Preparing for the Mission

[SCENE: The player sees Laika next to the supply vending machine and a landing simulator.]

## 1. Selecting the Landing Equipment

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x49;t's time for me to descend to Mars. I need the right equipment to land without being damaged.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x53;elect the three essential items: a parachute, a heat shield, and the Sky Crane landing system. Read the information about each item before making your choice.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x54;he parachute helps slow me down during descent. The heat shield protects me from the heat generated during atmospheric entry, and the Sky Crane system lowers the vehicle onto the surface.

**LAIKA — IF AN ITEM IS MISSING:&#x20;**&#x57;e're not ready yet. Check the equipment and make sure you've selected every required item.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x45;quipment complete. Starting landing simulator.

[GAME NOTE: Include distracting items, such as a toy or a balloon. The player cannot proceed until all the required equipment has been selected.]

## 2. Waiting for Landing Confirmation

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x4C;aika has been released from the spacecraft. Watch the landing simulator.

**PLAYER:&#x20;**&#x4C;aika, can you hear me? Confirm once you've landed.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x53;ignal sent. Waiting for a response...

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x43;ommunication between Earth and Mars has a delay of several minutes. You won't receive an immediate response.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x59;ou have two main options: send the same message again or wait for a response. You can also restart Laika's systems.

**LAIKA — IF THE PLAYER WAITS:&#x20;**&#x4D;essage received! I've landed safely. My systems are working normally.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x4C;anding confirmed. Level 2 completed.

**LAIKA — IF THE PLAYER REPEATS THE MESSAGE OR RESTARTS THE SYSTEMS:&#x20;**&#x54;here's no need to repeat the instruction or restart my systems. The signal simply needs time to arrive.

[GAME NOTE: Display a send button and a 30-second countdown. The player must wait without pressing the button again.]

# Level 3 — Exploring the Martian Surface

[SCENE: Laika is now on Mars and operating as a rover. A rocky landscape and a five-minute countdown are displayed.]

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x57;e did it, astronaut! I'm now on the surface of Mars. I'll begin exploring and collecting information for our mission.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x4F;bjective: Visit the marked locations and activate all the cameras to take photographs. Rock and soil samples will also be recorded.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x45;ach photograph will help us study the terrain. Keep an eye on the timer and complete the objectives before time runs out.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x54;ime available: five minutes. Photos remaining: [objective counter].

**SYSTEM — WHEN THE STORM BEGINS:&#x20;**&#x57;arning! A sandstorm is approaching. Visibility and power generation may be affected.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x44;ust can block the sunlight reaching my solar panels. We must stop all scientific activities, find shelter behind the rocks, and activate Low Power Mode.

**LAIKA — IF THE PLAYER TAKES SHELTER:&#x20;**&#x47;ood decision. I'll stay protected and reduce my power consumption until the storm passes.

**LAIKA — IF THE PLAYER KEEPS MOVING:&#x20;**&#x57;arning! The storm is affecting my systems and available power. I can't keep working under these conditions.

**SYSTEM — WHEN 3:30 MINUTES REMAIN:&#x20;**&#x54;emperature alert! The temperature is dropping rapidly. A period of extreme cold is approaching.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x54;he temperature may reach -125°C. I need to activate Hibernation Mode to protect my batteries and systems.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x41;ctivate Hibernation Mode and head toward the lava tunnel, where I can find shelter from the cold.

**LAIKA — IF SHE REACHES A SAFE LOCATION:&#x20;**&#x49;'m protected. My systems will remain in hibernation until conditions allow me to continue.

**LAIKA — IF SHE DOES NOT REACH SHELTER:&#x20;**&#x43;ritical alert! The extreme cold is damaging my systems. I can't continue the mission.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x49;f Laika completes the photographs and reaches the safe area before time runs out, she can proceed to the final level.

[GAME NOTE: The level lasts five minutes. When 3:30 minutes remain, the temperature begins to drop; the critical cold period lasts 45 seconds. The document mentions taking shelter near a lava tunnel and activating Hibernation Mode.]

# Level 4 — Exploring the Lava Tunnels

[SCENE: Laika reaches the entrance of a dark tunnel. The player must turn on her flashlight to see the path.]

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x57;e're almost done! These tunnels can provide shelter from the extreme conditions on the surface. But we must be careful: not every path is safe.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x54;urn on Laika's flashlight and find a safe route through the tunnel.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x4D;ove carefully and jump from one rock to another. Avoid falling; if you do, you'll have to return to the checkpoint at the entrance.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x54;ime available: four minutes. Find and interact with both objectives: the rock sample and the camera.

**LAIKA — WHEN THE SAMPLE IS FOUND:&#x20;**&#x52;ock sample recorded! This material will help us study the composition of the Martian terrain.

**LAIKA — WHEN THE CAMERA IS FOUND:&#x20;**&#x50;hotograph completed! We now have the visual record we needed.

**SYSTEM — IF THE PLAYER FALLS:&#x20;**&#x59;ou've lost your balance. Return to the last checkpoint and try again.

**LAIKA — WHEN BOTH OBJECTIVES ARE COMPLETED:&#x20;**&#x57;e did it! I have the sample and the photographs. It's time to return to the spacecraft.

[SCENE: A short return sequence begins. Laika is transported back to the spacecraft, where the player is waiting.]

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x53;cientific mission completed. Beginning return-to-Earth sequence.

**LAIKA:&#x20;**&#x54;hank you for helping me, astronaut. Together, we overcame the dangers of Mars and completed our mission.

**SYSTEM:&#x20;**&#x43;ongratulations! You have completed the training and the mission.
